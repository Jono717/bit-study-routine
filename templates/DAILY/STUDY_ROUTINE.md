## How a session runs

Start it with `/study` (or say "study session"). Claude then:

1. Reads `QUESTION_QUEUE.md` and takes the **next unstruck batch**, no more than 5 questions.
2. Asks them all at once, in plain language, no preamble.
3. I answer conversationally. Fragments are fine. "I don't know" is a valid answer and gets logged
   as an honest gap.
4. Claude logs my answers verbatim in `ANSWER_LOG.md`, files them as a raw block in the destination
   file named on the batch, and strikes the batch off the queue.
5. Session ends. No homework.

**Hard stop at 15 minutes.** Anything unfinished stays on the queue.

## Rules

- **Nothing gets invented.** If I did not say it, it does not go in the portfolio.
- **Claude does not write my portfolio.** It files my raw answers; I write them up in my own words.
- **Drafts only.** Nothing is written to the folder I submit from or share with my facilitator.
- **Workplace confidentiality.** The answer log may be specific. Anything filed into a draft uses
  neutral labels for internal systems, colleagues and budgets.
- **Skipping a day is not failure.** The queue does not expire.
- **"I don't know yet" and "ask my facilitator" both close a question.** They route it, which is
  progress.
