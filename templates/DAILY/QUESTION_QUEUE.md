## How to write a batch

- **One destination per batch:** the file and section the answers will be filed under.
- **Five questions or fewer.** Fifteen minutes goes quickly.
- **A "why" line** saying what the batch unblocks, so you know why it is worth answering.
- **Questions only you can answer:** your experience, your decisions, your judgement. Anything you
  could look up is reading, not a study-session question.
- **One fact per question.** "What did you do, and why, and what would you change?" is three.
